# Principles of Information Systems

> [!TIP]
> A team is proven in a project!

&nbsp;&nbsp;The course Principles of Information Systems (Princípy informačných systémov) focused heavily on independent teamwork using Camunda Modeler, where we explored business process modeling through BPMN diagrams and Petri Nets (Petriho siete).

&nbsp;&nbsp;For our main project, our team chose to automate a restaurant business, which required us to break down every single action performed by the guests, waiters, and chefs, and then weave them together into a seamless interaction workflow. The project was divided into three phases, and my specific responsibility was designing the individual BPMN diagrams for each participant in the process, ensuring all edge cases and interactions were covered. 

&nbsp;&nbsp;To conclude the project and demonstrate that our workflows operated flawlessly, we recorded a detailed video presentation explaining the system's logic and functionality. It was an intense experience that truly tested our time management, but our hard work paid off perfectly—we scored a maximum 50 out of 50, proving that a team is truly known in a project.
